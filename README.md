Overview
electronica India Tech Challenge

The electronica India Tech Challenge 2026 is a national innovation hackathon by Messe München, bringing together developers, hardware engineers, embedded systems experts, AI builders, students, professionals, startups, and innovators to solve real-world challenges shaping the future of electronics.

Whether you're building intelligent software, designing cutting-edge hardware, developing embedded systems, or creating AI-powered solutions, this challenge is your opportunity to innovate, collaborate, and make an impact.

Participants may register individually or as a team. While individual participation is welcome, we strongly encourage forming multidisciplinary teams with expertise in software, electronics, embedded systems, AI, hardware, and product development to create well-rounded, innovative solutions.

Rules
Eligibility

The hackathon is open to students, professionals, startups, researchers, and technology enthusiasts.
Participants must register through the official hackathon portal before the registration deadline.
Teams must comply with the team size requirements specified on the event page.
Team Formation

Teams may consist of 1–4 members.
Each participant can be part of only one team.
Project Requirements

All submissions must be developed during the hackathon period.
Existing open-source libraries and frameworks may be used; however, projects must demonstrate significant original work.
Teams must clearly disclose any pre-existing components or third-party assets used.
Projects should align with one or more of the published problem statements.
Submission Guidelines

Teams must submit the following before the deadline:
Source code repository
Project presentation (PPT or PDF)
Project documentation
Demo video (if applicable)
Any additional deliverables specified in the problem statement
Late submissions will not be considered.
Judging Criteria

Projects will be evaluated based on:
Innovation & Creativity
Technical Excellence
Problem Understanding
Feasibility & Scalability
User Experience & Design
Quality of Demonstration
Overall Impact
The judges' decision will be final and binding.
Code of Conduct

Maintain professional and respectful behavior.
Treat fellow participants, mentors, judges, and organizers with respect.
Avoid plagiarism, cheating, or misrepresentation.
Comply with all event communications and deadlines.
Any violation may result in disqualification.
Intellectual Property

Participants retain ownership of their intellectual property.
By participating, teams grant the organizers permission to showcase submitted projects, presentations, and demos for promotional and event-related purposes unless otherwise specified.
Disqualification

A team may be disqualified if it:
Submits plagiarized work.
Violates the Code of Conduct.
Provides false information.
Misses submission deadlines.
Fails to comply with the event rules.
Organizer Rights

The organizers reserve the right to:
Modify the event schedule if required.
Update rules or judging criteria when necessary.
Resolve disputes and make final decisions regarding the competition.
All official announcements made by the organizers during the hackathon shall be considered final
